Builds the deployments of a CKomposable solution (the "Deployment" folder next to its central ckonf.json): one generated builder and host per deployed App, published in the assets of the release.

Entry point: AppDeployment.RunAsync, called by the solution's Deployment/GenerateAssets.cs.

See the [README](https://github.com/signature-opensource/CK-StObj/blob/stable/CK.AppDeployment/README.md).
