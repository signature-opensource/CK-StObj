# CK.Engine.TypeSet

The types of the BinPaths for the CK engine: `BinPathTypeGroup.Run` groups the BinPaths of an `EngineConfiguration`
by the types they consider (their assemblies' types, their `Types` and `ExcludedTypes`, the `[AlsoRegisterType]`
closure) and computes their named type sets (`BinPathTypeGroup.TypeSets`).

See the [README](https://github.com/signature-opensource/CK-StObj/blob/stable/CK.Engine.TypeSet/README.md).
